    Method

    Simple steps for a relaxed dinner.

    1. Cook the pasta in salted water until just tender. Save a cup of cooking water before draining.
    2. Warm the olive oil and garlic in a pan. Stir in the lemon zest and a splash of pasta water.
    3. Toss with pasta, spinach, and parsley. Add a little more water until glossy, then finish with lemon juice.
