How scoring works

Daily attempts count once and can be resumed. Replays are practice only.

  • A win earns up to 100 points; a loss earns zero.
  • Efficiency and optional hints affect scores where allowed.
  • Unfinished attempts expire at household midnight.
  • Answers stay hidden on public scoreboards.
